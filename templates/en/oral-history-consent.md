## 2. How the recording will be handled

- The recording will be transcribed **on the project's own server**, with an AI program that runs there. **The audio is not sent to companies.**
- If I authorize "Public" or "Workshops" access, passages of the transcript may be sent, at the moment of a query, to the nonprofit AI service the project uses (Public AI, which runs the open Swiss model Apertus), only to generate the answer to that query.
- A staff member will review the transcript.
- Before it enters the archive, **I can read the transcript** and ask for corrections or removal of passages.

## 3. Access level I authorize (tick one)

- [ ] **Public:** anyone may consult it in the archive, and the AI assistant may use it in answers.
- [ ] **Workshops:** only in facilitated activities at the space.
- [ ] **Research:** only for research, on request to the archive council.
- [ ] **Anonymous:** my name and identifying details will be removed.

## 4. My rights

- I may **withdraw the recording and transcript** from the archive at any time, without giving a reason, by contacting the staff below.
- The interview **will not be sold** or given away to train commercial AI models without new authorization from me and from the archive council.
