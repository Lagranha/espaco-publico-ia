# Where your data goes — Espaço Público IA in [CITY]

*Text for the "About the AI" page and sign-up. Adapt it and have it reviewed under your data-protection law.*

| What | Where it is kept | For how long | Does it leave? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your account (name, email) | Our server, in Brazil | While the account exists | No |
| Your conversations with the assistant | Our server, in Brazil | 30 days, then deleted | Only the question and conversation history, to generate the answer (see below) |
| The archive (wiki) | Our server, in Brazil | Permanent | Only the passages used in each answer |
| Unanswered questions ("gaps") | Our server, with no data about you | Until curators handle them | No |

**How an answer is generated.** When you ask, our server searches the archive for relevant passages. The question, those passages and the conversation history are sent to the **Public AI Inference Utility**, a nonprofit service running the open model **Apertus**, built by Swiss public universities (EPFL, ETH Zurich and the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre). **Your name, email and IP address are not sent.**

**What Public AI states** in its terms (checked September 2026):

- it does not use data to train models, except for users who voluntarily join a specific program (we have not);
- it keeps technical logs for up to 90 days;
- it may share conversations with academic researchers, under data use agreements, for non-commercial research;
- it uses compute partners, including the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre, Exoscale and Amazon Web Services, and states it does not control what those partners do with the data.

So: **do not write personal data in your questions**.

**Your rights.** You can request access, correction or deletion of your data, and deletion of your account, at [email]. Data protection officer: [name].
