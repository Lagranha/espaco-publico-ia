## 1. What it is

The archive of Espaço Público IA in [CITY] ("the Archive") gathers documents, memories and solution records organized by the community. It is held under **community guardianship**: it belongs to those who produced it, is cared for by the Archive Council, and exists to serve the community.

Public-domain official documents (laws, official acts) remain in the public domain; this license covers the selection, organization, transcripts, records and other original content of the Archive.

## 2. What you may do

- Consult, read and quote passages of the Archive, citing the source.
- Use the information to solve problems, study, teach, report and do non-commercial research.
- Share specific passages with others, crediting the Archive and the author.

## 3. What you may not do without written permission from the Archive Council

- **Use the Archive, in whole or in part, to train, fine-tune, evaluate or improve artificial intelligence models**, including adding it to training datasets.

- Use the content commercially or resell it.
- Republish the whole Archive or substantial parts of it elsewhere.

## 4. Express text and data mining reservation

The rights holders **expressly reserve** text and data mining rights over the Archive, for all purposes, including training artificial intelligence systems. This reservation is also declared in machine-readable form (`tdm-reservation` and `X-Robots-Tag: noai` headers, and `robots.txt`).

## 5. Authors and contributors

Whoever writes a record or gives an interview keeps their author's rights and grants the Archive the right to keep, organize and make the content available under this license. Authors may ask for their content to be withdrawn at any time.

## 6. Use by the Espaço's own assistant

To answer queries, the assistant sends the question and relevant Archive passages to a nonprofit inference service (Public AI), whose terms state it does not use the data to train models. This use is authorized by this license and reviewed periodically by the Council.

## 7. Violations

Use contrary to this license will be treated as copyright infringement and may be reported publicly and to the competent authorities. The Archive contains markers that make it possible to identify misuse.
