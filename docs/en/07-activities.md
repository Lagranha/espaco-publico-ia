# 7. Critical-awareness activities to do from home

> The activities teach people to use AI critically and, at the same time, improve the archive: whoever takes part from home also contributes. A monthly online circle keeps it collective.

Publish activities in the wiki under `atividades/`, one page each, with short instructions and a comment space for answers.

## Activities

| Activity | How it works | Time | What it feeds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Check the source** | Ask the assistant a question, open the cited page and note in the comments whether the answer is correct, incomplete or wrong, and why | 10 min | Continuous quality evaluation |
| **Spot the error** | Each week, facilitators post an answer with a deliberately planted error. Find it and explain how | 10 min | Careful reading; shows in practice that AI makes mistakes |
| **Same question, different ways** | Ask the same thing three ways and compare the answers. What changed? Why? | 15 min | Understanding that answers depend on the question |
| **Translate for a neighbor** | Pick a passage of a law or call for proposals and rewrite it for a neighbor. Then compare with the assistant's summary | 20 min | Plain-language versions added to the archive |
| **Gap mission** | Pick a question from the gaps page and find the answer in real sources: official site, public phone line, someone who knows | 30+ min | New documents and records |
| **My record** | Tell how you solved a problem in the city, using the record template | 20 min | Solution records |
| **Inside the machine** | Short reading path: what a language model is; where data comes from; why it makes things up; what happens to what you type; why this archive is protected | 5 × 10 min | AI literacy |

## Guiding questions for any activity

- Where did this information come from? Can I check it?
- What was left out of the answer?
- If I asked differently, would the answer change?
- Is this an official document or someone's account?
- Who gains or loses if I believe this without checking?

## Monthly online circle (1 hour)

By video call, with a facilitator:

1. **Errors of the month (15 min):** the most interesting errors people found.
2. **Gaps (20 min):** the most frequent unanswered questions; who will look for what.
3. **New records (15 min):** what the community taught the community this month.
4. **One idea for next month (10 min).**

## No gamification

No points, badges, rankings or daily streaks. Those mechanics push toward engagement logic, which the project wants to avoid. Recognition is public credit, if the person wants it: "record written by Maria, from district X".
