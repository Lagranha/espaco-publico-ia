# 8. Governance, privacy and data protection

> The community decides what goes into the archive, who can use it and which AI the service uses. The server collects the minimum personal data and deletes conversations after 30 days.

## Anchor institution

The Espaço works best anchored in a stable, neutral institution: public library, university (extension project), technical institute or cultural foundation. It is legally responsible for the service, hosts or contracts the server and signs the agreement with Public AI.

## Archive council (5–7 people)

Representatives of the anchor institution, curators, facilitators and the community (residents' associations, schools, local businesses, faith and cultural groups). Monthly meeting, public minutes. Candidates and holders of political office do not take part.

The council decides on:

- archive entry criteria and withdrawal requests;
- the archive license and exceptional authorizations;
- the AI provider and model, based on tests and a six-monthly review of terms;
- usage limits and account approval;
- responses to violations (misuse, canaries found).

## Privacy

| Principle | How it is applied |
| --- | --- |
| Data minimization | Sign-up with name and email; no national ID; gaps stored without identification; daily limit with hashed ids |
| Retention | Conversations deleted after 30 days (`faxina`) |
| Transparency | "About the AI" page with the [data policy](../../templates/en/data-policy.md), including Public AI's caveats |
| International transfer | Passages and questions go to Public AI (US/Switzerland): disclosed at sign-up; users told not to write personal data |
| Data subject rights | Channel for access, correction and deletion; data officer named by the anchor institution |
| Oral history | [Consent form](../../templates/en/oral-history-consent.md) with access levels and right of withdrawal |

## Under-18s

Public AI's terms require users to be 18 or older. Until Public AI confirms otherwise in writing, minors should use the service only in activities facilitated by partner schools and institutions, under their responsibility.

## Neutrality

The Espaço serves residents of any political orientation. Prompts ask for balanced viewpoints; the terms forbid partisan propaganda; the archive accepts no campaign material. During elections, the council reviews activities with legal guidance.
